# MyWorkFlo — Answer every call. Book the job. Get a human when it matters.

MyWorkFlo is an AI front desk for HVAC and home-service teams. When you miss a call, it texts the customer back right away, books the visit if it's simple, and calls you if it sounds urgent. About 10 minutes to set up. No salesperson, no new software to learn.

Works alongside the tools you already use — business phone, text messages, Google Calendar, Jobber, Housecall Pro, email — even if that's just a phone and a paper calendar.

## What other options get wrong

- **No sales calls needed** — sign up and connect your calendar yourself. No demo, no waiting on a salesperson.
- **Booking is included, not an upsell** — some tools only send a text back; that still loses the job. MyWorkFlo actually books the appointment.
- **Spots real emergencies** — gas smell, flooding, sparks, burning smell: anything dangerous gets sent straight to a person, fast.
- **English and Spanish** — every plan answers callers in both languages.

## How it works

1. **You set it up** — service area, hours, appointment types, and which words mean "this is an emergency."
2. **It answers and checks** — responds to missed and after-hours calls, listens for urgent language, loops in a person when it matters.
3. **It books or hands off** — books the appointment and sends a confirmation, or flags it for your office if a person needs to decide.

## How much does it do on its own? You decide.

- **Draft** — it prepares, you approve. Staff approves every message before it's sent, except real safety emergencies, which send a locked, pre-approved safety script instantly.
- **Assisted** — it texts, you approve bookings. It can text back and check details itself; booking, dispatch, and pricing wait for your OK.
- **Autopilot** — it books on its own. Approved appointment types get booked automatically once the rules, schedule, and service area all match.

## Safety

Home service calls can be urgent. MyWorkFlo knows its limits: it won't guess at repairs, make up arrival times, or quote a price it isn't sure about — and it hands off fast the moment something sounds dangerous. You approve the scripts, every call is logged, and it works even if you have no website.
