# MyWorkFlo — Answer every call. Book the job. Get a human when it matters.

MyWorkFlo is an AI front desk for HVAC and home-service teams. Your calls still ring you first — miss one, and it texts the customer back in seconds, books the visit if it's simple, and alerts you if it sounds urgent. About 10 minutes to set up; texting real customers starts after a standard carrier registration for business texting (A2P), usually a few days. No salesperson, no new software to learn. 14-day free trial on every plan; no contract — cancel anytime.

Works alongside the tools you already use — business phone, text messages, Google Calendar, email — even if that's just a phone and a paper calendar.

## How it works

1. **You set it up** — service area, hours, appointment types, and which words mean "this is an emergency."
2. **It catches what you miss** — your calls still ring you first; the moment one is missed, it texts the customer back, watches for urgent language, and loops in a person when it matters.
3. **It books or hands off** — books the appointment and sends a confirmation, or flags it for your office if a person needs to decide.

## How much does it do on its own? You decide.

- **Draft** — it prepares, you approve. Staff approves every message before it's sent, except real safety emergencies, which send a locked, pre-approved safety script instantly.
- **Assisted** — it texts, you approve bookings. It can text back and check details itself; booking, dispatch, and pricing wait for your OK.
- **Autopilot** — it books on its own. Approved appointment types get booked automatically once the rules, schedule, and service area all match.
