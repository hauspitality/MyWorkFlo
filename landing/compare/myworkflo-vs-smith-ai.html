MyWorkFlo vs Smith.ai: which should an HVAC shop pick?

Short version: Smith.ai is an AI voice receptionist that answers calls for any kind of business, with escalation to its own live agent network. MyWorkFlo is a text-first AI front desk built only for HVAC and home-service teams — it recovers missed calls by texting back instantly, qualifies the job, books it, and pulls in your people when something's urgent. Both publish their prices. Which one fits depends on how you want the phone handled.

When is Smith.ai the better choice?

Pick Smith.ai if your callers should hear a voice pick up the phone — not receive a text — and your business isn't specifically in the trades. Its AI answers conversationally 24/7, it can hand a caller to a staffed live agent without involving your own team, and the free 25-call tier means you can try it with zero commitment. If you're a law office, a clinic, or a mixed portfolio of businesses, Smith.ai's industry-agnostic approach is the safer bet.

When is MyWorkFlo the better choice?

Pick MyWorkFlo if you run an HVAC or home-service shop and the real problem is the calls you miss — while driving, on a roof, or after close. Texting back beats voicemail because the customer gets an answer in seconds and can book without waiting on hold. The intake already speaks HVAC (no cooling, no heat, tune-ups, replacement estimates), a gas smell or burning smell triggers a locked safety script and an immediate handoff to your own people, every plan includes real calendar booking and English/Spanish, and the price is flat — it doesn't climb per call.
