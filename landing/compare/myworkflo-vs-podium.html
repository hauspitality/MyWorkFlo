MyWorkFlo vs Podium: focused front desk or full platform?

Short version: Podium is a lead management platform — reviews, payments, marketing, and a unified inbox — with a 24/7 "AI Employee" that works across calls, text, email, and social. MyWorkFlo does one job: it's an AI front desk for HVAC and home-service teams that recovers missed calls by text, qualifies the job, books it, and escalates emergencies to your people. The honest question isn't which is "better" — it's whether you're buying a platform or solving one problem.

When is Podium the better choice?

Pick Podium if you want one vendor running the whole revenue side of the front office — review generation, payment collection, marketing campaigns, and messaging — in a single inbox, especially across multiple locations. Its AI Employee reaching across calls, text, email, and social is genuinely broader than what MyWorkFlo does, and if you're already consolidating tools, a platform purchase through a sales team may be exactly what you want.

When is MyWorkFlo the better choice?

Pick MyWorkFlo if you already have tools you like and one specific leak to fix: the calls that ring out while you're driving, on a job, or closed. You can see the price on the pricing page, start yourself in about 10 minutes without a demo, and get trade-native behavior out of the box — no-cooling and no-heat qualification, a locked safety script the moment someone mentions a gas smell, English and Spanish on every plan, and control modes that decide how much it does without you. It deliberately doesn't do reviews, payments, or marketing.
