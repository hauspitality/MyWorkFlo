MyWorkFlo vs Avoca: which fits your shop's size?

Short version: these two mostly serve different shops. Avoca is a full AI front office for the trades — always-on agents answering every call, text, and chat, plus outbound campaigns and CSR coaching — sold through a demo, with deep ServiceTitan integration, to operations from single locations up to PE-backed platforms. MyWorkFlo is a self-serve, text-first front desk for 2–20 tech shops with published flat pricing. The honest question is your size and how you want to buy.

When is Avoca the better choice?

Pick Avoca if you're a larger operation — dedicated CSRs, maybe multiple locations — running on ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or FieldRoutes, and you want one platform to answer with a voice on every channel, run outbound campaigns, and coach your CSR team. At that scale Avoca is genuinely the more complete product, and a sales-led rollout with deep CRM integration is what you should want.

When is MyWorkFlo the better choice?

Pick MyWorkFlo if you're the shop Avoca's process is heavy for: 2–20 techs, no CSR department, and a specific leak to fix — the calls that ring out while everyone's on a job. You can see the price ($119–$599/mo flat), start yourself today without a demo, keep your phone setup untouched, and get HVAC-native behavior out of the box: no-cooling/no-heat qualification, locked safety scripts on emergency keywords with handoff to your team, and English/Spanish texting on every plan.
