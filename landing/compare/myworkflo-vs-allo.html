MyWorkFlo vs Allo: add a layer, or replace your phone system?

Short version: these are different categories that overlap on one feature. Allo is a business phone system — seats, shared lines, call administration — with an AI receptionist add-on that answers day and night and books from your calendar. MyWorkFlo leaves your phone setup completely alone and adds a recovery layer on top: when a call is missed, it texts the customer back, qualifies the job HVAC-style, and books it. The decision is really whether you want to switch phone systems.

When is Allo the better choice?

Pick Allo if you were going to replace your business phone setup anyway. If you want shared lines, team seats, call administration, and an AI receptionist in one bill, Allo's published pricing is the lowest entry point on our alternatives list — a few seats plus the $32/mo AI agent can land well under $150/mo. Getting that price means moving your phones onto their system, which is exactly the right move for some teams.

When is MyWorkFlo the better choice?

Pick MyWorkFlo if the phone setup you have works fine and the problem is just the calls nobody answers. Nothing migrates: your number still rings your phones first, and one conditional call forwarding setting sends only the calls you miss to your MyWorkFlo number. You also get behavior a general-purpose receptionist doesn't have — no-cooling/no-heat qualification, locked safety scripts the moment someone mentions a gas smell, handoff to your own on-call person, and English/Spanish texting on every plan.
