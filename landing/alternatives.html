What is each alternative, and when should you pick it?

Smith.ai

AI voice receptionist · Free tier, then $150/mo (Pro) or $500/mo (Enterprise) · smith.ai

Smith.ai's AI Receptionist answers calls 24/7 with an AI voice, includes AI scheduling (Calendly and others), and can escalate to Smith.ai's live agent network. Pricing is published and there's no long-term contract: a free plan covers 25 calls/month, Pro is $150/mo, and Enterprise is $500/mo. It serves every industry, not just the trades.

Choose Smith.ai if… you want an AI that talks to callers by voice and occasionally hands off to Smith.ai's own live agents, and you don't need trade-specific behavior. The free tier makes it easy to try.
Choose MyWorkFlo if… you want HVAC-native handling — emergency keywords like a gas smell trigger a locked safety script and a human handoff to your team — with booking, English/Spanish, and flat published pricing on every plan.

Podium

Lead management platform + AI Employee · Pricing not published · podium.com

Podium's "AI Employee" works across phone calls, text, email, and social from one inbox, responds 24/7, and books directly on your calendar. It sits inside Podium's broader platform — reviews, payments, and marketing — built for service businesses from single locations to multi-location operations. Podium does not publish pricing; plans go through a sales conversation.

Choose Podium if… you want one platform to run reviews, payments, marketing, and messaging together, you have multiple locations, and you're comfortable buying through a sales process.
Choose MyWorkFlo if… you already have your tools and want exactly one problem solved — missed calls answered, qualified, and booked — at a published price you can start on yourself in about 10 minutes.

Avoca

AI front office for the trades · Pricing not published · avoca.ai

Avoca is the most complete trades-specific platform on this list: always-on AI agents answer every call, text, and chat and book jobs 24/7, it's a ServiceTitan Certified App with deep CRM integration (plus Housecall Pro and FieldRoutes), and it layers on outbound campaigns and CSR coaching. Avoca serves 1,000+ service leaders, from single locations to PE-backed multi-location platforms. Pricing is not published — you book a demo.

Choose Avoca if… you're a larger operation — multiple CSRs, maybe multiple locations — running on ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or FieldRoutes, and you want a full front-office platform including voice AI and coaching. At that scale, Avoca is genuinely the more complete product.
Choose MyWorkFlo if… you're a 2–20 tech shop that doesn't want a sales-led rollout. You set it up yourself, pay a flat published price, and get the core job done: missed calls recovered, emergencies escalated, jobs booked.

Allo

Business phone system + AI receptionist · From $32/seat/mo annual ($45 month-to-month) + $32/mo per AI agent · withallo.com

Allo is a phone system first — seats, shared lines, call administration — with an AI receptionist add-on that picks up day and night, reads your calendar, books jobs, and can transfer to the right person. It serves many phone-driven industries (home services among them, alongside sales teams, medical, legal, and more). Pricing is published: Business is $32/seat/mo billed yearly ($45 month-to-month), and the AI receptionist is $32/mo per agent.

Choose Allo if… you want to replace your whole business phone setup — lines, seats, and routing — and get an affordable AI receptionist as part of that switch. On published entry price, it's the cheapest option here.
Choose MyWorkFlo if… you want to keep your existing phone number and setup untouched and add a recovery layer on top, with HVAC-specific qualification and emergency triage rather than a general-purpose receptionist.

Retell AI

Build-your-own voice AI platform · $0.07–$0.31/min pay-as-you-go, $2/mo per number · retellai.com

Retell isn't a front desk product — it's the platform you'd use to build one. You compose your own voice AI agent from templates, APIs, and webhooks, and pay per minute of AI conversation plus $2/mo per phone number. There's a free credit to start and custom-priced enterprise support.

Choose Retell if… you have developer resources and want full control over exactly how a custom voice agent behaves, and you'd rather pay per minute than a flat subscription.
Choose MyWorkFlo if… you're running an HVAC company, not a software project. The qualification flows, emergency keywords, booking rules, and bilingual intake are already built.

Ruby

Human virtual receptionists · $250/mo (50 min) to $1,725/mo (500 min) · ruby.com

Ruby is the opposite bet: 100% live, trained human receptionists answering 24/7 — nights, weekends, and holidays — with call handling, scheduling, bilingual support, and no setup or activation fees. You pay for receptionist minutes: $250/mo for 50 minutes up to $1,725/mo for 500 minutes.

Choose Ruby if… your callers should always reach a warm human voice and your call volume fits a per-minute budget. For high-trust, low-volume phones, a real person is hard to beat.
Choose MyWorkFlo if… your problem is volume and speed — every missed call answered by text within moments, at a flat price that doesn't climb with minutes, with your own team looped in when it matters.

What does MyWorkFlo actually do differently?

MyWorkFlo is an AI front desk built specifically for HVAC and home-service teams. It responds to missed and after-hours calls instantly by text, qualifies the job with trade-native questions, books real calendar appointments on every plan (including the $119/mo Starter), answers in English and Spanish, and treats safety differently: emergency keywords like a gas smell trigger a locked, pre-approved safety script and an immediate human handoff. You choose how much it does on its own — Draft, Assisted, or Autopilot — and setup is self-serve in about 10 minutes. It deliberately does not try to be your phone system, your CRM, or your marketing platform.
