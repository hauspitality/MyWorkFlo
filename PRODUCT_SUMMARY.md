# MyWorkFlo Product Summary

MyWorkFlo is a self-serve AI front desk for HVAC and home-service businesses.

It helps contractors answer missed and after-hours calls, respond instantly by text, qualify service requests, detect urgent issues, book approved appointments, and hand off risky or unclear situations to a real person.

## Core Promise

Answer every call. Book the job. Get a human when it matters.

## Who It Is For

MyWorkFlo is built for small home-service teams, starting with HVAC companies, especially businesses with 2 to 20 technicians that lose leads while the owner, dispatcher, or technicians are driving, on job sites, or closed after hours.

The product is designed for practical contractors who need a working front desk quickly, not an enterprise demo funnel.

## Main Product Wedge

MyWorkFlo stacks four market gaps into one offer:

- Self-serve setup in minutes
- Calendar booking included from the first paid tier
- Emergency-keyword triage with human handoff
- English and Spanish intake

## Key Workflows

- Missed-call text-back
- After-hours AI receptionist
- HVAC service qualification
- Emergency-keyword triage
- Calendar booking
- Estimate follow-up
- Bilingual caller intake
- Human handoff for urgent, unsafe, angry, unclear, or exception-based cases

## What Makes It Different

MyWorkFlo is not a generic AI receptionist or a broad contractor platform. It is a revenue-recovery layer that works beside the tools a contractor already uses.

It is trade-native, meaning the intake flow understands HVAC-specific situations like no cooling, no heat, tune-ups, replacement estimates, burning smells, gas concerns, and other emergency signals.

It does not give unsafe repair guidance, invent arrival times, quote unsupported prices, or book outside approved business rules.

## Pricing Direction

Three tiers, mapped directly to fit (technician count) and automation level (the product's own draft / assisted / autopilot control modes):

| | Starter — $119/mo | Growth — $299/mo | Pro — $599/mo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fit | Solo operators | 2–10 techs | 10–20 techs / multi-location |
| Automation | Draft mode — AI drafts every reply, owner/dispatcher approves & sends | Assisted — routine replies auto-send, bookings need one-tap approval | Autopilot — books jobs end-to-end; only emergencies/exceptions escalate to a human |
| Seats | 1 (owner) | Up to 3 (owner + dispatcher + 1 tech) | Unlimited staff |
| Volume | Soft cap (~150 conversations/mo) | Higher cap (~500/mo) | Unlimited |
| Calendar booking | Included | Included | Included |
| Bilingual (EN/ES) | Included | Included | Included |

Calendar booking is included from the first paid tier, not gated as an upsell.

Pricing is anchored to one recovered job paying for the month, and spacing across tiers is intentionally non-linear: a 10–20 tech shop has several times the revenue at risk of a solo operator, so Pro should read as a rounding error against payroll rather than a flat per-seat markup. Growth is the expected default choice for most self-serve buyers. (These prices — $119/$299/$599 — are the shipped canon on the landing page, JSON-LD, and in-app plan picker as of 2026-09-22.)

An annual option at roughly two months free (Starter effective ~$99/mo annual) is planned as a cash-flow/retention lever, independent of the tier structure above.

- Trustworthy
- Fast
- Operational
- Human-aware
- Trade-native
- Practical
- Calm under pressure

The visual identity should avoid futuristic AI cliches. It should feel like dependable field-service software: clean, direct, professional, and easy for a contractor to trust.
